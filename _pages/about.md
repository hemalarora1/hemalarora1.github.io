## Selected Work {#work}

{: .entries}
- **Multiply Labs** · Robotics Software Engineering Intern <span class="meta">· Jun–Dec 2025</span><br>
  Robot learning and motion generation for automated cell-therapy manufacturing. Trained Diffusion Policy and ACT models for millimeter-scale cartridge insertion on a UR10e, and built collision-free motion generation in NVIDIA Isaac Sim.
- **Stanford Autonomous Underwater Vehicle Team** · Robotics Software Engineer <span class="meta">· Jan 2026–present</span><br>
  Building the autonomy stack for a robotic submarine competing in RoboSub 2026: stereo-depth object detection, EKF localization, SE(3) trajectory generation, and 6-DOF control. Advised by Prof. Oussama Khatib.
- **Maximum Likelihood RL for Robotic Manipulation** · [report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1k0ZR1L6nJsZQnbiY_WSD_H_6xZp6qzfe/view?usp=sharing)<br>
  First application of MaxRL to sparse-reward 7-DOF manipulation: a goal-conditioned behavior-cloning policy with hindsight relabeling, fine-tuned with MaxRL and benchmarked against RLOO.
- **Diffusion Test-Time Compute** · [report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1zy5rgdC6C9AS1JMK_EjxwjcMNlYkbjEE/view?usp=sharing)<br>
  An inference-time scaling framework for diffusion models that searches over denoising trajectories, enabling guided image generation without retraining.
- **Prizmatic** · Founding Engineer <span class="meta">· Jan 2025–Mar 2026</span> · [website](https://www.prizmatic.ai/)<br>
  An LLM-driven platform for systematic biomedical reviews with Stanford HAI and Stanford Medicine, replicating human-led reviews with 96.1% accuracy in under an hour; partnered with the WHO.
- **1st Place, OpenAI Prize at TreeHacks** · [Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/fable-br8e1d)<br>
  A generative storytelling platform that adapts plot, visuals, and sound in real time to EEG brainwave data.
